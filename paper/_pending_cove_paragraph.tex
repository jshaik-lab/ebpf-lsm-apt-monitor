\noindent\textbf{CoVe ablation.}
On the {{n_paired}} TI-aided DARPA windows, paired evaluation with and without
the CoVe verification loop (\S\ref{sec:cove}) shows that CoVe corrects
{{raw_wrong_cove_correct}} windows that the raw LLM mis-classified and
downgrades {{raw_correct_cove_wrong}} windows that the raw LLM got right; an
exact two-sided McNemar's test gives $p\,=\,{{mcnemar_pvalue}}$.
With CoVe enabled the system achieves
F1\,$=$\,{{cove_f1}} (95\% CI [{{cove_f1_lo}}, {{cove_f1_hi}}]) vs.\
F1\,$=$\,{{raw_f1}} without. The mean per-window hallucination rate ---
defined as the fraction of LLM evidence references that fail to match any
\texttt{event\_id} in the IPG window --- is {{mean_hallucination_rate}};
across all windows, {{total_verified_claims}} claims were grounded against
real \texttt{event\_id}s and {{total_retracted_claims}} were retracted as
unverifiable. The retracted set is the operationally-relevant measure of
SENTINEL's anti-hallucination contribution: every enforcement action under
the CoVe-on configuration is backed by at least one verified
\texttt{KernelEvent.event\_id}.
